Terms of Service

HeatCheck Poster is a private publishing tool operated by Heat Check Clips. It uploads short video clips made by Heat Check Clips to the Heat Check Clips accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram (@heatcheckclipshq).

Who can use it

The tool is used only by the Heat Check Clips team to post to its own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign up or connect an account.

Content

  • Every clip is reviewed and approved by a person before it is posted.
  • Posts follow the community guidelines and terms of each platform, including TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • Clips credit the original streamer in the caption. If a creator or rights holder asks us to remove a clip, we remove it.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is for internal use. Heat Check Clips may change or stop it at any time.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: heatcheckclips@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

HeatCheck Poster only connects to the accounts owned by Heat Check Clips. It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.

What the tool accesses

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok account (account ID and display name), to confirm which account it posts to.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • The access token TikTok issues after we log in.

How it is stored

The access token is stored only on the Heat Check Clips computer that runs the tool. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

What it does not do

  • It does not read followers, messages, comments or any other user's data.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking on this page.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from our computer. Requests: heatcheckclips@gmail.com
